Third-party software and data in MoonInfo

Moon maps (data/moon_color.jpg and data/moon_height.png) and the icon
  NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit
  (https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720), from data by the Lunar Reconnaissance
  Orbiter camera (LROC, Arizona State University) and laser altimeter (LOLA)
  teams.  The icon is from NASA's "Moon Phase and Libration" imagery.  NASA
  imagery is not copyrighted; credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio.

Everything else MoonInfo uses (the window and controls, reading the
maps, the location service and the internet lookup) is part of Windows.
